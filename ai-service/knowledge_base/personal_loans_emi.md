# Personal Loans & EMI Planning Guide

## Equated Monthly Installment (EMI)
An Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is a fixed payment amount made by a borrower to a lender at a specified date each calendar month. EMIs consist of both principal and interest components.
* **EMI Formula**:
  - $EMI = [P \times R \times (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N - 1]$
  - Where $P$ is Principal loan amount, $R$ is monthly interest rate, and $N$ is loan tenure in months.
* **Amortization Schedule**: Shows the split of each EMI between principal and interest. In the early tenure of a loan, interest makes up the major portion of the EMI, while principal repayment increases in the later years.

## Loan Interest Rates: Fixed vs Floating
* **Fixed Interest Rate**: The interest rate remains constant throughout the entire tenure of the loan.
  - **Advantage**: Certainty in monthly budgets; no inflation shocks.
  - **Disadvantage**: Does not decrease even if market rates drop.
* **Floating Interest Rate**: The interest rate is linked to a benchmark rate (like RBI's Repo Rate) and changes periodically.
  - **Advantage**: If market interest rates decrease, your EMIs or loan tenure will drop.
  - **Disadvantage**: EMIs can rise unexpectedly if the RBI raises rates to curb inflation.

## Credit Score (CIBIL Score)
In India, CIBIL score is the most common credit rating used by lenders to assess loan eligibility.
* **Score Range**: Ranges between 300 and 900.
* **Excellent Score**: A score of 750 or above is considered excellent and qualifies you for quick approvals and lower interest rates.
* **Factors Affecting Credit Score**:
  - **Repayment History**: Paying EMIs and credit card bills on time (major factor).
  - **Credit Utilization Ratio**: Keeping credit card balances below 30% of their limits.
  - **Credit History Length**: Maintaining older credit card accounts.
  - **Credit Mix**: Having a healthy balance of secured loans (home/car) and unsecured loans (personal/credit cards).
